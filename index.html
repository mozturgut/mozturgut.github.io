My research centers on using single-cell RNA sequencing to characterize the complex cellular environment of the spinal cord following traumatic injury. By profiling thousands of individual cells — neurons, astrocytes, microglia, oligodendrocytes, macrophages, and infiltrating immune cells — I aim to identify key molecular drivers of secondary injury and potential therapeutic targets for neuroprotection and regeneration.

Current focus: Neutrophil and macrophage heterogeneity in acute spinal cord injury — dissecting transcriptional sub-states to understand pro- vs. anti-inflammatory dynamics at single-cell resolution using CELLCALL and edgeR.

Research Projects

Investigating the single-cell transcriptomic landscape of spinal cord injury — from acute immune responses to chronic glial remodeling — using state-of-the-art computational tools.

Neutrophils

Neutrophil Heterogeneity in Acute SCI

Leveraging CELLCALL and Seurat to characterize transcriptional sub-states of infiltrating neutrophils in the first 24–72 h post-injury. Identifying pro-resolution vs. tissue-damaging sub-populations.

CELLCALL edgeR Seurat
Astrocytes

Reactive Astrogliosis at Single-Cell Resolution

Mapping astrocyte activation states along the injury gradient using UMAP trajectory analysis and pseudotime inference. Distinguishing A1 neurotoxic from A2 neuroprotective phenotypes in vivo.

Seurat Monocle3 SingleR
Microglia

Microglial Polarization & Cell–Cell Communication

Dissecting how resident microglia shift between homeostatic and disease-associated states post-SCI. Using CellChat to map ligand–receptor interactions with infiltrating macrophages and OPCs.

CellChat Seurat ggplot2
Macrophages

Macrophage Sub-state Dynamics in SCI Repair

Profiling peripheral macrophage infiltration and resident microglial proliferation using time-series scRNA-seq at 1, 3, 7, and 14 days post-injury. Exploring pro-regenerative macrophage sub-populations.

edgeR clusterProfiler topGO
Oligodendrocytes

OPC Differentiation Trajectory Post-SCI

Reconstructing oligodendrocyte precursor cell (OPC) lineage trajectories after injury to understand the failure of remyelination and identify gene regulatory programs controlling OPC maturation.

Monocle3 Seurat DESeq2
Neurons

Neuronal Transcriptomics & Survival Markers

Identifying transcriptional signatures of surviving vs. degenerating neurons in the injured spinal cord. Volcano plot and GO enrichment analysis to nominate neuroprotective candidate genes for therapeutic validation.

EnhancedVolcano topGO GEOquery
